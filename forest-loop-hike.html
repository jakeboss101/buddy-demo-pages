Your First Forest Day Hike: An Easy 5-Mile Loop Guide

September 21, 2026 · 6 min read

You don't need fancy gear or a mountain to fall in love with hiking. A gentle forest loop of about five miles is the perfect first step: shady, quiet and done by lunch. Here's how to make your first one great.

The loop at a glance
  • Distance: about 5 miles (8 km) round trip
  • Elevation gain: 600 ft (180 m), gentle grades
  • Time: 2.5 to 3 hours at an easy pace
  • Difficulty: easy to moderate

Pick the right trail

Look for a loop under six miles with less than 1,000 feet of climbing. Check recent trail reports for mud or closures, and choose a trailhead with parking and a map board. Loops are nicer than out-and-back trails because every step is new.

What to pack

  • Water: at least 1 liter per person, 2 on warm days
  • Snacks: trail mix, an apple, a sandwich for the halfway point
  • Layers: a light jacket; forests are cooler than the parking lot
  • Shoes: comfortable sneakers with grip are fine for dry trails
  • Basics: phone with an offline map, sunscreen, a small first-aid kit

Pace yourself

Start slower than feels necessary. If you can't hold a conversation, ease off. Take a five-minute break every hour, and drink before you feel thirsty. Uphills go better with short steps.

Trail manners

  • Hikers going uphill have the right of way.
  • Stay on the trail to protect plants and soil.
  • Pack out everything you bring, even orange peels.
  • Keep music in your headphones so everyone can hear the birds.

Safety basics

Tell someone where you're going and when you'll be back. Check the weather, and turn around if storms roll in. Start before noon so you're never racing the sunset.

That's it. Lace up, start small, and let the trees do the rest.
