Weekend Lake Trip: The Only Packing List You Need

October 12, 2026 · 5 min read

A weekend at a lake is the easiest trip there is: no flights, no tight schedule, just water, trees and a good book. The trick is packing light but not forgetting the few things that matter. Here's the list.

Trip at a glance
  • Length: 2 nights, Friday evening to Sunday afternoon
  • Best months: late spring to early fall
  • Book a campsite or cabin early. Summer weekends fill up fast.

Sleep

  • Tent with rain cover, or a cabin booking
  • Sleeping bag rated 10°F (5°C) below the forecast low
  • Sleeping pad and a small pillow

Clothes

  • Swimsuit and a quick-dry towel
  • Warm layer for the evening; lakes get chilly after sunset
  • Rain jacket, hat and an extra pair of socks
  • Sandals for the beach and closed shoes for trails

Kitchen

  • Camp stove, fuel and a lighter
  • One pot, one pan, a spatula and a sharp knife
  • Cooler with ice packs
  • Reusable plates, cups and utensils
  • Biodegradable soap and a sponge

On the water

  • A life jacket for everyone, even strong swimmers
  • Dry bag for your phone and keys
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses; water reflects the sun

Don't forget

  • Headlamp with fresh batteries
  • Small first-aid kit and bug spray
  • Trash bags: pack out everything you bring
  • Offline map of the area

A simple plan

Friday: arrive before dark, set up camp, make an easy dinner.
Saturday: paddle in the calm morning water, swim after lunch, campfire at night.
Sunday: sunrise coffee by the shore, a short hike, pack up by noon.

Leave your campsite cleaner than you found it, and the lake will be just as nice next time.
