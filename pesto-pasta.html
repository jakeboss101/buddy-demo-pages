20-Minute Pesto Bow-Tie Pasta with Cherry Tomatoes

October 3, 2026 · Serves 4

This is the pasta I make when the fridge is almost empty and everyone's hungry. The pesto blends while the water boils, and the tomatoes add a sweet, juicy pop. It's just as good cold the next day as a pasta salad.

At a glance
  • Prep: 10 minutes · Cook: 10 minutes
  • Vegetarian · nut-free if you skip the pine nuts
  • Keeps 3 days in the fridge

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) farfalle (bow-tie pasta)
  • 2 packed cups fresh basil leaves
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
  • 1/4 cup pine nuts, lightly toasted
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • 1 1/2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 handfuls baby arugula or mixed greens
  • Salt and black pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta. Bring a big pot of well-salted water to a boil and cook the farfalle until al dente, about 10 minutes. Save 1/2 cup of the pasta water before draining.
  2. Make the pesto. While it cooks, blend the basil, Parmesan, pine nuts, garlic and lemon juice. With the blender running, pour in the olive oil until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Toss. Return the drained pasta to the pot. Add the pesto and a splash of pasta water, and stir until glossy.
  4. Finish. Fold in the tomatoes and greens off the heat so they stay fresh. Top with more Parmesan.

Tips and swaps

  • No pine nuts? Walnuts or sunflower seeds work great.
  • Add grilled chicken or white beans to make it heartier.
  • Don't cook the pesto. Heat turns basil dark and bitter.
Nutrition (per serving, about)

540 calories · 17 g protein · 68 g carbs · 24 g fat
